    About

    I've spent twenty years in web development, and most of that time was spent explaining complicated things in plain language — to clients, to teams, and from conference stages. Narration is a natural extension of that. My voice is male, mid-forties, with an educated central-Indiana accent — the neutral American sound newscasters practice. People have told me for years I should be on the radio. I finally listened.

    I'm drawn to nonfiction, business, and technology titles, where my background lets me read with real understanding rather than just pronouncing the words. When the material calls for it, I can bring energy and excitement to a read. Listening to audiobooks is one of my favorite pastimes, so I come at this with a clear sense of what makes a read work.

    I record from a home studio in Indiana, I hit deadlines, and I communicate clearly throughout a project.
